Frequently asked questions.

Learn more about the Divity concept and how group payments are intended to work.

What is Divity?

Divity is designed to help groups organize bill splitting before making a shared payment. The goal is to make each person's share clear and reduce the need to collect money from friends afterward.

How is Divity intended to work?

A group organizes a bill, agrees on how the total should be split, and each participant contributes their share. The intended experience uses a group payment method to settle the total, subject to the product's actual availability and terms.

Can we split a bill in different ways?

The product concept includes options such as splitting evenly, by item, or by percentage. Available split methods depend on the functionality released in the Divity product.

Does everyone need to agree on their share?

Group members should review and agree on their assigned amounts before the group completes a payment.

Does Divity support tax and tip?

The intended bill-splitting experience can account for tax and tip. The exact calculation options and their availability should be confirmed in the live product.

Does the contact form send a message?

The contact form on this static website opens the visitor's default email application with a message prepared. It does not submit information to a server.

Is Divity currently available?

Availability, supported locations, eligible users, and product features may change. Contact the Divity team for current launch and availability information.

How can I contact Divity?

Visit the Contact page to find the contact email and prepare a message.

Where can I read the privacy policy and terms?

Read the Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, and Legal Information pages. These documents must be reviewed and completed before the website is published.
